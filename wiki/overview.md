# Project Overview

[[DEVERTown]] is a broad nine-area multiplayer campus experience with working foundations for exploration, learning, social presence, customization, quests and minigames. The current strategic constraint is not feature count; it is whether those systems form a trustworthy, legible reason to act and return.

The approved product contract positions the experience as a digital clubhouse for FPT University Da Nang students and FU-DEVER members, with priorities Social 50%, Learn 30% and Play 20%. It declares targets of D1 >= 40%, D7 >= 20%, 12–18 minute sessions and monthly return >= 30%; repository telemetry does not yet independently verify the supplied baseline.

The first slice from [[retention-audit-2026-09-04]] is now implemented on `develop`: progression uses a local-first authoritative path with serialized account sync, and a Daily Goal HUD surfaces the next useful action plus honest recovery state. [[RetentionFramework]] defines the measurement and ethical guardrails. The next product gate is privacy-safe instrumentation, followed by the Social-first v0.5.0 work: guest migration, Hall of Fame and a consent-led voice experiment.
