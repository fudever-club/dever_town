## Purpose

Use a combined MDA, Self-Determination and HEART lens to decide which DEVER TOWN enhancements deserve implementation.

## Framework

- **MDA:** trace each mechanic to a runtime dynamic and intended player feeling.
- **Competence:** progress and feedback must be understandable and reliable.
- **Autonomy:** let players select a useful next goal rather than forcing one checklist.
- **Relatedness:** make real people, shared activities and reconnection visible.
- **HEART:** define goal, signal and metric before scaling a feature.

## Journey

`Activation → Meaningful action → Return → Social loop`

The behavioral north-star is **Meaningful Weekly Return (`MWR-7`)**: a player performs a meaningful action on at least two distinct days in a seven-day window. The Product Spec separately targets **Monthly Return Rate (`MRR-30`) >= 30%**; the distinct names prevent the previous `MWR` acronym collision.

Product targets are D1 >= 40%, D7 >= 20% and average session length 12–18 minutes. The supplied DAU/WAU/onboarding baseline remains declared rather than telemetry-verified.

## Guardrails

- No lost/duplicate progress.
- No manipulative streak-loss messaging.
- No map/portal regressions.
- No inaccessible or mobile-obscured primary actions.
- No telemetry expansion without a privacy contract.
