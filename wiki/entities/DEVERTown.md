## Overview

DEVER TOWN is a browser-based 2D multiplayer digital clubhouse for FU-DEVER and FPT University Da Nang students, with an expansion path for prospective students and partner developers. Its value proposition combines club community, student utility, learning spaces, customization and arcade play across nine functional areas.

## Current Product Thesis

The approved product order is Social 50%, Learn 30%, Play 20%. The game can retain players when its breadth becomes a legible loop: a useful reason to enter, visible progress, reliable reward, player choice and social reconnection. More rooms alone remain lower priority.
